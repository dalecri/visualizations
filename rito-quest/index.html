Rito Quest

A gift from Cris. You play as Rito in a pocket-sized Labelle, Quebec.

Four friends are in town: Kate, Kyle, Corbin and Phil. Each one holds a key and has a small quest for you. Kate lost her vape. Kyle is counting salamanders.

Collect all four keys, wake the portal, and open the chest it gives up.

Along the way: four cats, an egg that hatches into Fern (who later grows into Thicket), and 15 stars to find.
